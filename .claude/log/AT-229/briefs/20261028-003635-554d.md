How should the contract suite express "a handover" when neither cell simulation knows a handover step?

## Summary
AT 229 gives the cell-simulation seam its own interface module and a contract suite run against both cell simulations (the model-level stand-in and the crc cell simulation).
Contract case 3 asks that "after gripping, releasing, a handover and a tool change" the snapshot at the next inspection shows the same holds and mounted tools.
Neither simulation has a handover process: both change who holds a part only for "gripping at the supply" and "releasing", and the step expansion says "v1 plans no holding and no handover".
With the recommendation the case pins that the hold moves from robot 1 to robot 2 across release and supply grip; a real handover step, when a later build adds one, replaces this sequence in the same test.
The alternative is to leave the handover out of case 3 until that build; that pins less today.
