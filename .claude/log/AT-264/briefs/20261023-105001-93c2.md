When B1 does not know where a group screw sits, should it treat the screw as close to every other screw, so that it never runs at the same time as another screw of its group?
I recommend treating it as close, because the maintainer's story asks that close screws never run together, and an unknown distance cannot show that they are far apart.

## Summary
AT 264 lets two screws of the same group run at the same time only when they are at least 20 cm apart. B1 reads the distance from the screw positions it is given (the centre of each screw's mesh in the product model, in metres). B1 now gets these positions as a planner setting; the demo runs pass them, and other callers (the planner contract tests, a future app wiring) may not. With "unknown counts as close", such a caller still gets out-of-order screws and the finish-first rule, but never two screws of one group at once. The other answer, "unknown counts as far", would pair screws that might be neighbours, and only the cell simulation's check would stop them.
