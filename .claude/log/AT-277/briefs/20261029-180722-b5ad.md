In the model-level stand-in, should a machine operation keep its robot busy for the machine's cycle time?
I recommend yes for now, because an operation needs a robot share to be scheduled and the cell-simulation sibling (AT 279) times it properly as a wait.

## Summary
AT 277 adds the machine operation (rework milling in the DMG) to the model-level stand-in. Every operation in a plan needs at least one robot share; the request is refused otherwise. The build gives the machine operation one share of Robot 1 and stretches it to the cycle time ("its own, else the travel-time key `rework_milling_cycle_time`, the skill default"), so Robot 1 waits during milling. This makes plan makespans conservative: Robot 1 cannot do other work during the cycle. AT 278 (the run controller ends a segment at a machine operation) and AT 279 (the cell simulation times it as a wait) decide the final behaviour; changing it here touches one helper.
