## Summary
AT 249 ports Hao's desk localisation, which fits the 265 System 28 desk holes in the camera image.
Seen from a partial view, the hole pattern repeats every 50 mm diagonally, so the fit can land one repeat off; the first simulated test was 70 mm off.
The build now lists every shifted position that agrees with the desk edges the camera sees, and picks one by markers, or else "nearest to the nominal desk pose".
This is right only while the nominal desk pose is within about 35 mm of the real one; otherwise the desk, and the clamps located from it, sit one repeat off.
The alternative is to require markers, or a view of two desk edges, and to report the desk as not found otherwise.
