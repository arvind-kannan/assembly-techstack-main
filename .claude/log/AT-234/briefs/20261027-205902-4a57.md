## Summary
AT 234 builds one removal plan core. It decides once whether a removal plan failed, the unit removal direction and whether the part is really gone. The gym's adapter module goes away, and the ticket says the builder "wires the capabilities to the core directly". Today the builder passes `plan_func=plan_screwing_adapter` from that adapter module. The capabilities call `plan_func(assembly, operation)`, as ADR-0004 fixes it, and the unit tests plug their own planners in through that same form. Option A (recommended): the core gets two functions in that form, and the capability translates every planner's output through the core's judgement, so no test planner and no ADR contract changes. Option B: the capabilities call the core's MRTAMP-shaped functions (assembly, target, cost) themselves. That changes the ADR-0004 contract, every planner a test passes in, and the demo tool in tools/.
